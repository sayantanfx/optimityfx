## Brand at a glance
- **Font:** Inter (headings weight 800, tight tracking; body in Muted grey)
- **Signature accent:** Cyan `#00D4FF`
- **Base background:** Near-black `#07090C`
- **Primary gradient:** `linear-gradient(135deg,#00D4FF,#1E90FF)`
- **FX accent:** Red `#FF3B57` / warm gradient for the logo mark

## Logo usage
- Prefer the **SVG** (`optimityfx-logo-mark.svg`) or **PNG** for anything digital — PNG has a transparent background.
- Use the **JPEG** only where transparency isn't supported (it has a solid background).
- Place the logo on dark backgrounds (the brand is a dark theme). Keep clear space around it; don't stretch or recolor.
