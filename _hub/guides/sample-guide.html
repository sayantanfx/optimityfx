Delivery SOP

From brief to final export — the standard OptimityFX process. Keep client approval gating between each stage.

  1. Story & ScriptLock the concept, message and hook. Get written client approval before any visuals.
  2. Mood BoardColors, references, style frames. Confirm direction with the client.
  3. Voice & MusicPick VO + track that matches brand tone. Approve before generation.
  4. Scene GenerationGenerate AI footage per the approved board. Flag re-rolls early.
  5. EditAssemble timing, transitions, rhythm. Internal QA pass first.
  6. Color GradeApply the signature OptimityFX cinematic finish. Export grade reference still.
  7. Final DeliveryExport all requested formats (Reels / Stories / YouTube). Collect final payment.
